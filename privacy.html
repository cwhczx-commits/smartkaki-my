Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This is a general-purpose starting point, not legal advice — have it reviewed by a local lawyer once SmartKaki MY becomes a registered business, especially before collecting any personal data directly.

1. What we currently collect

SmartKaki MY does not require an account, login, or any personal details to browse deals. We don't collect names, emails, or payment information on this site.

2. Hosting and technical data

This site is hosted on Vercel, which may automatically log standard technical data (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance purposes, under Vercel's own privacy policy. We also load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads, under Google's privacy policy.

3. Affiliate links and third-party platforms

When you click a deal, you're taken to a third-party merchant (e.g. Shopee, Lazada) through our affiliate partner, Involve Asia. Involve Asia's tracking link may set a cookie to attribute the referral. Once you land on the merchant's site, their own privacy policy governs any data they collect from you — we don't see or store your purchase details.

4. Telegram channel

Joining our Telegram channel is subject to Telegram's own privacy policy, not this one. We don't receive your personal Telegram data by you joining the channel.

5. Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site grows — for example, if we later add newsletter sign-ups or accounts. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@smartkaki.my or via our Telegram channel.
